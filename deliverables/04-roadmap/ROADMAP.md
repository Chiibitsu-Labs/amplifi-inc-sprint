and a date ~ otherwise it's just ideas. Owners confirmed by Michele; dates
shift only by her call.

**The 90-day success bar (Michele's own words):** *"consistent quality, no
AI slop, reports aligned with branding, evolving insights as data points of
a client accumulate over time."* Everything below serves that sentence.

## Phase 3 ~ Ongoing (no end date, by design)

| Rhythm | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | capchecker 3-tap check-in (08:00) · summary to Michele (10:00) | all · auto |
| Per cycle | delivery-log: **open at start** (`delivery-log.md`'s own touch 1 ~ whoever owns this client's queue, the lead analyst or Rica, since the eventual ship-of-record isn't always known yet at period start and ownership can change mid-cycle), **ship-touch + insight-log entry at delivery, finalize at acceptance** (~20–30s each) | **queue owner** (Rica or lead analyst) opens the row; **delivering analyst** (whoever actually ships, refreshed at touch 2 if handed off) does the ship/finalize touches |
| Per cycle (feedback moment) | insight-log: **update the `Client reaction` line, and the WHOLE entry if the feedback was substantive** (`insight-log.md`'s own second touch, plus its conditional third) ~ at the presentation, or whenever the client actually reacts by email/call, never inferred from silence. Without an assigned owner for this specific touch, `Client reaction` sits at `pending` indefinitely and a late substantive correction never reaches the entry `amplifi-insights` reads as trusted history next period (~20–30s, same order of cost as the other touches, just at a different, less predictable moment in the cycle) | **delivering analyst** (present for or told about the reaction) |
| Weekly (Fri) | promotion pass: learnings → corpus | Rica (+ improve skill) |
| Weekly (Fri, same sitting) | silent-acceptance scan, TWO writes per finalized row, not one: (a) delivery-log ~ any `delivered`/`revising`/`revising (reopened)` row with a NON-BLANK `Last Sent` whose `Last Sent` + 5 business days has passed with no revision request finalizes to `accepted` now (a blank `Last Sent` ~ touch 3's revision-request-just-arrived state, fix not yet resent ~ is never eligible here regardless of how old the row looks, per `delivery-log.md` touch 3/4) ~ plain markdown doesn't do this on its own, nothing revisits a row automatically after 5 quiet days, so without this scan a silently-clean report can sit unresolved indefinitely (a resent REOPENED report included, or it would stay `revising (reopened)` forever instead of returning to `accepted`, Codex catch, 2026-07-19) and its workflow status/`Client reaction` field would stay stuck instead of finalizing ~ **not that its rework goes uncounted in the meantime, since `revising (reopened)` is already trusted alongside `accepted` for gate (a)/(b)'s rework math** (`INSTRUMENT.md` §5a/§5b), so this scan's finalization matters for closing out the workflow and reaction field, not for making that row's rounds eligible in the first place (Codex catch, 2026-07-19: an earlier draft called the rework calculation "accepted-only," which stopped being the actual scope once `revising (reopened)` was added as an equally-trusted status); (b) **insight-log ~ the SAME row's matching `insight-log.md` entry, if its `Client reaction` is still `pending`, updates to `no feedback / administratively accepted`** (`insight-log.md`'s own required distinction, never left as bare `pending` once the silence window closes ~ the feedback-touch row above only fires when the client actually reacts, so nothing else in this rhythm ever performs this specific write for the no-reaction path, leaving entries `pending` forever and reading as a broken feedback loop that never actually broke, Codex catch, 2026-07-19). Piggybacks on the promotion pass's existing Friday sitting rather than adding a new habit, **but the Friday cadence alone isn't tight enough to trust blindly at walkthrough time** ~ a report sent Tuesday clears its 5-business-day window the FOLLOWING Tuesday, up to three days before the next Friday scan catches it, so a row that's genuinely eligible can still read `delivered`/`revising` (excluded from the rework denominator) and its insight-log entry can still read stale `pending` for those days (Codex catch, 2026-07-19) ~ see the monthly walkthrough row below, which closes this gap at the one moment it actually matters | Rica |
| Monthly | walk the router chain by hand (Instrument §5a) and log the routing decision ~ starts at 2.2 (Sep 11), continues on this cadence whether or not 2.3's automation has shipped. **Run the SAME silent-acceptance eligibility sweep (row above) immediately before reading the logs for this walkthrough, not just whatever the last Friday scan happened to catch** ~ the Friday cadence alone can leave a genuinely eligible row still sitting `delivered`/`revising`/`pending` for up to a few days, and reading rework/on-cadence data through that gap risks a stale, upward-skewed rounds-per-report or a missed accepted-row denominator seat at the exact moment the router decision gets made (Codex catch, 2026-07-19) | Michele (+ Rica, immediate pre-walkthrough sweep) |
| Quarterly | threshold calibration against real distributions · corpus health check (stale files, unfilled frames) | Michele + Chii |

The quarterly calibration is a standing working session ~ thresholds are
living numbers, and the instrument evolves as Amplifi does.

## The brief's own Definition of Success (checked at the 90-day review)

1. Can any analyst produce a client-ready report to the same standard,
   without tribal knowledge? → *corpus + skills (Phases 1–2)*
2. Does the COO have a near-real-time view of capacity and output per
   deliverable? → *capchecker + delivery logs + panel (2.2–2.3)*.
   **Cost per deliverable specifically is not yet in scope** ~ the feeds
   capture effort hours, not a labor rate or cost formula; "cost" from the
   brief's own definition of success would need that input added
   deliberately (a rate table, or a simple hours × blended-rate calc),
   which nobody has asked for yet. Flagged here so it's a known gap, not a
   silent miss ~ revisit with Michele if cost visibility becomes a real ask.
3. Are existing subscriptions used to documented capability? → *skills on
   Claude Enterprise; Drive as vault; no new platforms*
4. Is there a living knowledge base growing with every engagement? →
   *capture loop (1.5–1.6)*
5. Is there a clear, agreed signal for when AI augmentation is no longer
   sufficient and a hire is warranted? → *the router, live and in manual
   use from 2.2 (Sep 11), automated by 2.3 ~ the whole point*
